Privacy Policy

PDFSmart Tools respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information and files may be handled when you use our website and online document tools.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

PDFSmart Tools is designed to let visitors use available online tools without creating an account.

When you use our website, technical information may be processed by our hosting provider or other service providers. Depending on their services, this information may include IP address, browser type, device information, request details, and server logs.

We do not ask users to provide personal information merely to browse the website. If you contact us, we may receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your enquiry.

2. File Processing

Some PDFSmart Tools features require you to select or upload a file so that the requested operation can be performed. Depending on the tool, processing may take place on our server or through another service used by the feature.

Please review the behaviour of the relevant tool before uploading documents. Do not upload confidential or sensitive files unless you are comfortable with the processing required to provide that service.

3. Temporary Files and Deletion

Our PDF-to-Excel conversion feature temporarily stores the uploaded PDF and generated Excel file on the server while processing the conversion.

After the Excel data has been prepared for download, the system attempts to delete the temporary PDF and Excel files. If deletion fails, the system attempts cleanup again. The system may also attempt to remove certain abandoned temporary files older than one hour when a subsequent conversion starts.

Other tools may process files in memory rather than saving separate temporary files. File handling depends on the specific tool and its implementation.

We aim to retain uploaded files only for as long as necessary to provide the requested service. However, we cannot guarantee that every deletion attempt will always succeed or that all technical copies, backups, or logs are immediately removed.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed during normal website operation may be used to:

  • Provide the requested website tools and features.
  • Maintain website performance and functionality.
  • Identify errors and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Protect the website against misuse and security threats.
  • Respond to enquiries sent to us.
  • Understand usage and improve the website, where applicable.

We do not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the website or provide a requested service.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. Depending on the service, these technologies may support website functionality, remember preferences, measure usage, or deliver and measure advertisements.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features. Where required by applicable law, consent will be requested before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

PDFSmart Tools may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense or other advertising partners.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to deliver advertisements, limit repeated advertisements, measure performance, and help determine which advertisements may be relevant.

Google's use of advertising data is governed by Google's own policies and settings. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

You can review available advertising controls through Google My Ad Center .

7. Analytics, Hosting and Third-Party Services

Our website relies on hosting and technical infrastructure to deliver its pages and tools. These providers may process technical information as part of providing, securing, and maintaining their services.

If analytics or other third-party services are enabled, those services may collect information such as pages visited, browser and device details, approximate location, referral information, and general usage data, in accordance with their own policies.

Third-party providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies when using services linked to or integrated with our website.

8. Third-Party Links

PDFSmart Tools may contain links to external websites. We do not control the privacy practices, content, or security of those websites. Visiting an external website is subject to that website's own policies and terms.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to maintain the security and proper functioning of our website and its services. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action where necessary.

11. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can choose whether to use our online tools, manage cookies through your browser, and decide what files you upload. You may also contact us with questions or requests concerning information associated with your use of the website.

We will review privacy-related requests and respond as appropriate under applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, tools, service providers, or applicable requirements. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled on PDFSmart Tools, please visit our Contact Us page.
