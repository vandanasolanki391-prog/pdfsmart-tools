Privacy Policy

PDFSmart Tools respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using our online PDF tools.

1. Information We Collect

PDFSmart Tools is designed to allow users to use PDF and document tools without creating an account. Depending on the tool you use, files may be selected or uploaded by you for processing.

We do not intentionally collect personal information from your documents unless it is required for a specific feature or service.

2. File Processing

Files submitted through our tools are processed for the specific purpose of providing services such as PDF conversion, merging, splitting, compression, OCR, translation, or other document-related operations.

Users should avoid uploading documents containing highly sensitive or confidential information unless they are comfortable using the relevant online service.

3. How We Use Information

Information that may be collected through normal website operation may be used to operate, maintain, improve, secure, and troubleshoot PDFSmart Tools and its services.

We do not intentionally sell or misuse users' personal information.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to provide advertisements and measure advertising performance.

Google may use information collected through advertising technologies in accordance with its own privacy policies and applicable laws.

6. Analytics and Third-Party Services

We may use third-party analytics or website services to understand how visitors use PDFSmart Tools, identify technical problems, and improve the user experience.

These third-party services may collect information such as browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited, referring pages, and general usage information.

7. Third-Party Links

PDFSmart Tools may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party websites before using them.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security and proper functioning of our website and services. However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security of information transmitted over the internet.

9. Children's Privacy

PDFSmart Tools does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our website, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.

10. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use our online tools and whether to allow cookies through your browser settings. You may also stop using the website at any time.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices. Any updated version will be published on this page with the revised content.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information on PDFSmart Tools, please visit our Contact Us page.
