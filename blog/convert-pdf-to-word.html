How to Convert PDF to Word

Extract selectable text from PDF files and export it into an editable Word document.

Introduction

Converting a PDF into Word allows you to edit contracts, reports, resumes, invoices and other documents without recreating them from scratch.

Why Convert PDF to Word?

  • Edit extracted text in a Word document.
  • Reuse existing documents.
  • Save time on manual typing.
  • Share editable files with your team.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Open the PDF to Word tool.
  2. Upload your PDF document.
  3. Click Convert.
  4. Wait for processing to finish.
  5. Download the editable DOCX file.

Tips for Better Results

  • Use clear, high-quality PDFs.
  • Scanned PDFs contain images of text and need OCR before this tool can extract their text.
  • Review headings, tables, page breaks and formatting after conversion.

Common Questions

Is it free?

Yes. PDFSmart Tools provides free PDF to Word conversion.

Will formatting stay the same?

The tool focuses on extracting selectable text, so page layout, tables, images, columns and other formatting may change. Review and edit the Word document after conversion.

Can I convert scanned PDFs?

Not directly. This tool extracts selectable text and does not perform OCR on scanned page images. Use an OCR tool to recognize the text first, then try converting the resulting searchable PDF.
