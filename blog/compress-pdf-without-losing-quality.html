How to Reduce PDF File Size Online

Learn how PDF optimization works and how to check the result after processing.

Why Compress a PDF?

Large PDF files can be difficult to email, upload and share. PDF optimization may reduce a file's size by adjusting or reorganizing parts of the document. The amount of reduction depends on the contents and structure of the original PDF, and some files may show little or no size change.

How to Compress a PDF

  1. Open the PDF compression tool.
  2. Upload your PDF file.
  3. Select an available option if the tool displays one. The options may use similar optimization methods.
  4. Click Compress PDF.
  5. Download the optimized file.

Benefits

  • Faster uploads and downloads
  • Easier email sharing
  • Saves storage space
  • Can make some PDFs easier to upload and share

Tips for Best Results

  • Compare the original and optimized file sizes; not every PDF can be made smaller.
  • Open the optimized PDF and check text, images and page layout before sharing it.
  • Keep an original copy of important documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will quality be reduced?

The tool optimizes the PDF structure and may preserve the original pages and visual appearance. However, a reduction in file size is not guaranteed. Always open the resulting PDF and check that its text, images and layout are acceptable before sharing it.

Is the tool free?

Yes, PDFSmart Tools offers free PDF compression.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. It works on Android, iPhone, tablets and desktop browsers.
